[italic]By receipt, review, or implied knowledge of this document, the signatory enters into a perpetual and irrevocable non-disclosure agreement with NanoTrasen Interstellar. This agreement is effective immediately upon exposure and may only be voided by Central Command or an authorized corporate adjudicator.[/italic]

[head=3][bold]COVERED INFORMATION[/bold][/head]
This agreement applies to all classified, restricted, proprietary, anomalous, biological, biohazardous, esoteric, or cognitively compromising data encountered during the course of NanoTrasen employment, including but not limited to:

• Research logs, anomalous entity data, experiment outcomes  
• Bluespace signatures, transit telemetry, void-event records
• Personnel, medical, psychological, or disciplinary files  
• Security protocols, armament inventories, and command-level directives  
• Any information whose classification exceeds the signatory’s clearance  
• Any material designated sensitive retroactively by the Corporation.  
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]OBLIGATIONS OF THE SIGNATORY[/bold][/head]
[bold]SUBJECT TO THIS AGREEMENT, THE SIGNATORY SHALL:[/bold]

1. Refrain from disclosing any restricted, classified, or sensitive material whether obtained intentionally or through incidental exposure.  
2. Avoid discussion of such information with any party lacking equal or superior clearance, including coworkers, external agencies, family, or personal acquaintances.  
3. Deny the existence of this agreement, its contents, or any connected operations when questioned without valid authentication or emergency override.  
4. Comply fully and without resistance with any memory suppression, verbal oath, behavioral conditioning, or cognitive inhibitor protocols issued alongside this agreement.  
5. Submit to interviews, debriefings, or psychological scans as mandated by Corporate Security or Internal Stability Enforcement.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]ENFORCEMENT & CONSEQUENCES[/bold][/head]
Violation of this agreement constitutes a Class-C Integrity Breach under the NanoTrasen Internal Stability Enforcement Charter and is subject to the full extent of corporate authority.

[bold]CONSEQUENCES MAY INCLUDE[/bold]:

• Immediate termination of contract.
• Permanent revocation of interstellar work eligibility.  
• Civil, criminal, and financial liability.  
• Indefinite detainment or containment procedures.  
• Memory partitioning, cognitive restructuring, or involuntary compliance.  
• Execution, with or without post-mortem data extraction authorization.  

[italic]The nature of what you know is not your concern.  
The extent of your silence is our command.[/italic]
